Ice Cream!

Here is my favorite ice cream recipe. I usually make a doulbe batch of it. This recipe is to create cream which will still need to be frozen.

List of ingredients to be heated in saucepan:

  • 500g heavy cream
  • 250g whole milk
  • 100g dark chocolate chips

List of ingredients to be combined in seperate bowl:

  • 6 arge egg yolks
  • 80g sugar
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 3g vanilla extract

Ice Cream!

This recipe will make about two pints.

List of ingredients to be heated in saucepan:

  • 1000g heavy cream
  • 500g whole milk

    • The total amount of liquids is 1500g. If you want to experiment with eveporated milk or happen to only have 2% milk in the fridge just make sure the total amount equals 1500g.

  • 200g dark chocolate chips

List of ingredients to be combined in seperate bowl:

  • 12 egg yolks
  • 160g sugar
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 6g vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Combine cream, milk, and chocolate chips. Heat until chocolate chips are fully dissolved and combined. Temperature should not exceed 185 degrees. After ingredients are dissolved and combined remove from heat source and move onto next step.
  2. Combine egg yolks, sugar, cocoa powder, and vanilla extract in a bowl.
  3. Slowly add cream mixture into bowl one scoop at a time. Continue doing this until cream and bowl combined.
  4. Heat combined mixture until it reaches 192 to 195 degrees F. Stir constantly. This creates the custard.
  5. Put custard in a bowl and place in freezer for 4 hours.
  6. Place custard in ice cream maker and make ice cream custard.
